Residue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Residue ("the app") is a personal music journal built by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and what is and isn't collected.

Summary

  • Residue does not have its own servers or user accounts.
  • Your journal entries are stored locally on your device.
  • The app does not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.
  • The app does not display ads.

Information the app accesses

Now-playing / music data. Residue connects to the Last.fm API (and, in future versions, Spotify) using your account username to look up the track, artist, and album artwork for your current or recent listening activity. This request goes directly from your device to the music service's API — Residue does not route it through, or store it on, a server it controls.

Journal entries. Anything you write in Residue — notes, interpretations, privacy settings on an entry — is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly use the app's share feature.

Sharing. If you choose to share an entry (for example, to Instagram Stories or TikTok), Residue generates an image on your device and hands it to your phone's native share sheet or photo library. That image is only sent wherever you personally choose to send it.

Device storage and media access. The app requests permission to save images to your photo library only when you use the share/save feature.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account registration, email, or password.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data to third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Residue's only outside connections are to the music services you link (Last.fm, and Spotify in future), solely to display your listening activity. Each of those services has its own privacy policy governing the data you share with them directly:

Children's privacy

Residue is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect information from children.
